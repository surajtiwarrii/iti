MMV — Mechanic Motor Vehicle

Government Industrial Training Institute, Karnailganj, Gonda
🏠 Home Overview Syllabus Career Gallery Admission
NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Automobile & Vehicle Repair

MMV trade trains students in engine repair, vehicle maintenance, transmission systems, braking, steering, and auto electrical — preparing them for jobs in garages, service centers, and automobile companies.

Eligibility
10th Pass
Duration
2 Years
Fees
₹780 / Year

Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Engine & Fuel System (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Introduction to Motor Vehicle & Safety
  • Engine Fundamentals (Petrol & Diesel)
  • Engine Dismantling & Assembly
  • Cylinder Head & Valve Mechanism
  • Fuel System — Carburettor & MPFI
  • Diesel Fuel Injection System
  • Cooling System (Water & Air)
  • Lubrication System
  • Engine Tune-Up & Testing
  • Engine Scanning & Diagnostics
  • Turbocharger Engine Maintenance
  • Emission Norms & Pollution Control
2. Transmission & Suspension (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Clutch — Types & Repair
  • Gear Box — Manual & Automatic
  • Propeller Shaft & Universal Joint
  • Rear Axle & Differential
  • Front Axle & Wheel Alignment
  • Suspension System (Leaf & Coil Spring)
  • Independent Suspension
  • Wheel Balancing
  • Tyre Fitting & Rotation
3. Braking & Steering System (Semester 3)
  • Hydraulic Brake System
  • Drum Brake & Disc Brake
  • Brake Relining & Adjustment
  • ABS (Anti-Lock Braking System) Basics
  • Steering Geometry & Alignment
  • Rack & Pinion Steering
  • Power Steering Maintenance
  • Hand Brake Adjustment
4. Auto Electrical & Electronics (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Battery — Charging & Testing
  • Starting System & Starter Motor
  • Charging System & Alternator
  • Ignition System (Conventional & Electronic)
  • Lighting System & Wiring
  • Dashboard Instruments & Gauges
  • Car AC System
  • Relay & Fuse Operations
  • OBD / Engine Scanning
  • EV Basics — Electric Vehicle Introduction
5. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy
  • Basics of Computer
  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety
  • Workshop Safety Rules
  • Fire Safety & First Aid
  • Fuel Spillage Handling
  • PPE Usage
Entrepreneurship
  • Garage Business Setup
  • Customer Service
  • Spare Parts Management
Quality & Productivity
  • Quality Control in Repair
  • 5S Housekeeping
  • ISO Basics

Admission Process

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in MMV.

Apply Online at scvtup.in →
1
Visit scvtup.in — official UP ITI admission portal
2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose MMV as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
