RAC — Refrigeration & Air Conditioning

Government Industrial Training Institute, Karnailganj, Gonda

NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Refrigeration & Air Conditioning

RAC trade trains students in installation, servicing and maintenance of refrigerators, AC units, cold storage and commercial cooling systems — preparing them for jobs in growing AC/refrigeration service industries.

Eligibility
10th Pass
Duration
2 Years
Fees
₹780 / Year
Certification

Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basic Refrigeration Practice (Semester 1)
  • Safety Practices & PPE
  • RAC Tools & Equipment
  • Refrigerants & Their Properties
  • Pipe Fitting, Brazing & Flaring
  • Basic Refrigeration Cycle
  • Compressors — Types & Working
2. Domestic Refrigeration (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Domestic Refrigerator — Construction & Working
  • Refrigerator Servicing & Gas Charging
  • Thermostat & Control Components
  • Water Coolers & Visi-Coolers
  • Deep Freezers — Servicing
  • Troubleshooting Common Refrigerator Faults
3. Air Conditioning Systems (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Window AC — Installation & Servicing
  • Split AC — Installation & Servicing
  • Cassette & Tower AC Systems
  • AC Gas Charging & Leak Testing
  • Electrical Wiring of AC Units
  • AC Remote & PCB Controls
4. Commercial & Industrial RAC (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Central Air Conditioning Systems
  • VRF / VRV Systems Overview
  • Cold Storage Systems
  • Ducting & Ventilation Systems
  • Car AC Systems
  • Energy Efficiency & Inverter Technology
  • Eco-friendly Refrigerants & Environment Norms
5. Maintenance & Tools (All Semesters)
  • Preventive Maintenance Schedules
  • Use of Gauges, Manifolds & Vacuum Pumps
  • Refrigerant Recovery & Recycling
  • Customer Service & Job Costing
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy

  • MS Office Basics

  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety

Admission Process

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in RAC.

Apply Online at scvtup.in →
1
Visit scvtup.in — official UP ITI admission portal
2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose RAC as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
