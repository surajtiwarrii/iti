NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career as a Skilled Fitter

Fitter trade trains students in fitting, assembling and maintaining machine parts, mechanical structures and equipment using hand tools, power tools and machine shop practices — preparing them for jobs in manufacturing, maintenance and engineering industries.

Eligibility
10th Pass
Duration
2 Years
Fees
₹780 / Year
Certification
NCVT / SCVT
Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Workshop Practice & Safety (Semester 1)
  • Safety Practices & PPE
  • Hand Tools & Their Uses
  • Marking & Layout Tools
  • Filing, Chipping & Sawing
  • Basic Fitting Practice
  • Measuring Instruments — Vernier & Micrometer
2. Bench Work & Fitting (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Drilling, Tapping & Threading
  • Riveting & Soldering
  • Sheet Metal Work
  • Assembly & Fitting of Components
  • Surface Finishing Techniques
  • Reading & Interpreting Job Cards
3. Machine Shop Practice (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Lathe Machine — Basic Operations
  • Drilling Machine Operations
  • Shaping & Milling Basics
  • Grinding Operations
  • Power Tools — Usage & Safety
  • Cutting Tools & Tool Geometry
4. Welding & Fabrication (Semester 3)
  • Arc Welding — Basics
  • Gas Welding & Cutting
  • Soldering & Brazing
  • Fabrication of Simple Structures
  • Welding Safety Practices
5. Maintenance & Engineering Drawing (All Semesters)
  • Reading Engineering Drawings
  • Limits, Fits & Tolerances
  • Preventive & Predictive Maintenance
  • Pneumatics & Hydraulics — Basics
  • Pipe Fitting & Plumbing
  • Bearings, Belts & Couplings
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy
  • Basics of Computer
  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety
  • Workshop Safety Rules
  • Fire Safety & First Aid
  • PPE Usage
  • Housekeeping (5S)
Entrepreneurship
  • Business Setup Basics
  • Customer Service
  • Self-Employment Planning
Quality & Productivity
  • Quality Control Concepts
  • 5S Housekeeping
  • ISO Basics

Admission 2026-27

1

2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose Fitter as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
