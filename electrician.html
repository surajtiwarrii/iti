NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Electrical Wiring & Maintenance

Electrician trade trains students in wiring, circuit installation, electrical machines, industrial maintenance and renewable energy basics — preparing them for jobs in domestic, commercial and industrial electrical sectors.

Eligibility
10th Pass
Duration
2 Years
Fees
₹780 / Year
Certification
NCVT / SCVT
Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basic Electrical Practice (Semester 1)
  • Safety Precautions & PPE
  • Electrician's Hand Tools
  • Wires, Cables & Their Joints
  • Soldering & Crimping Techniques
  • Basic Electrical Measurements
  • Earthing Practices
  • First Aid & Electric Shock Treatment
2. Wiring & Domestic Installation (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Domestic Wiring Systems
  • Switches, Sockets & Fittings
  • Distribution Boards & MCB/RCCB
  • Fluorescent & LED Lighting Circuits
  • Fan, Calling Bell & Geyser Wiring
  • Earthing & Lightning Protection
  • Estimation & Costing of Wiring Jobs
3. DC & AC Machines (Semester 2 & 3)
  • DC Generators & Motors
  • Transformers — Construction & Testing
  • Single Phase & Three Phase Induction Motors
  • Motor Starters (DOL, Star-Delta)
  • Alternators & Synchronous Machines
  • Winding of Small Motors
  • Motor Fault Diagnosis & Rewinding
4. Industrial Wiring & Control (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Industrial Wiring & Cable Sizing
  • Three Phase Distribution Systems
  • Battery Charging & Maintenance
  • Basic PLC & Automation Concepts
  • Solar Power Systems — Installation Basics
  • Renewable Energy Devices Overview
  • Power Factor Improvement
  • Wiring Estimation & Electrical Drawing
5. Maintenance & Safety (All Semesters)
  • Electrical Maintenance of Machines
  • Protective Devices & Circuit Breakers
  • Testing Instruments (Multimeter, Megger)
  • Electrical Safety Rules & Standards (IE Rules)
  • Trouble Shooting of Electrical Circuits
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy
  • Basics of Computer
  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety
  • Workshop Safety Rules
  • Fire Safety & First Aid
  • PPE Usage
  • Housekeeping (5S)
Entrepreneurship
  • Business Setup Basics
  • Customer Service
  • Self-Employment Planning
Quality & Productivity
  • Quality Control Concepts
  • 5S Housekeeping
  • ISO Basics

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in Electrician.

2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose Electrician as preferred trade

Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
