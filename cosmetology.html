NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Beauty & Cosmetology

Cosmetology trade trains students in skin care, hair care, beauty treatments, bridal makeup and salon management — preparing them for jobs in beauty salons, spas, and self-employment opportunities.

Eligibility
8th Pass
Duration
1 Year
Fees
₹780 / Year
Certification
NCVT / SCVT
Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basics of Beauty & Hygiene (Semester 1)
  • Introduction to Cosmetology Trade
  • Salon Hygiene & Sanitation
  • Tools & Equipment Used in Salon
  • Skin Types & Skin Analysis
  • Basic Facial Treatments
  • Personal Grooming & Etiquette
2. Skin Care & Facial Treatments (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Cleansing, Toning & Moisturizing (CTM)
  • Facial Massage Techniques
  • Threading, Waxing & Bleaching
  • Manicure & Pedicure
  • Acne & Skin Problem Care
  • Use of Beauty Equipment (Steamer, Vaporizer)
3. Hair Care & Styling (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Hair Types & Hair Analysis
  • Hair Cutting Techniques
  • Hair Styling & Blow Drying
  • Hair Coloring & Highlights
  • Hair Treatments (Spa, Smoothening)
  • Hair Straightening & Perming Basics
4. Makeup & Bridal Services (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Basic & Party Makeup
  • Bridal Makeup Techniques
  • Contouring & Highlighting
  • Eye Makeup & False Lashes
  • Saree Draping & Pre-Bridal Care
  • Mehendi Application Basics
5. Salon Management (All Semesters)
  • Salon Setup & Management
  • Client Consultation & Communication
  • Product Knowledge & Inventory
  • Pricing, Costing & Record Keeping
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy
  • Basics of Computer
  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety
  • Workshop Safety Rules
  • Fire Safety & First Aid
  • PPE Usage
  • Housekeeping (5S)
Entrepreneurship
  • Business Setup Basics
  • Customer Service
  • Self-Employment Planning
Quality & Productivity
  • Quality Control Concepts
  • 5S Housekeeping
  • ISO Basics

Admission Process

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in Cosmetology.

Apply Online at scvtup.in →
1
Visit scvtup.in — official UP ITI admission portal
2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose Cosmetology as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
