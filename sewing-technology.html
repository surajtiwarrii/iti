NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Sewing & Apparel Production

Sewing Technology trade trains students in sewing machine operations, garment stitching, tailoring and basic apparel production techniques — preparing them for jobs in garment factories and self-employment.

Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Introduction to Sewing Trade (Semester 1)
  • Safety Practices in Sewing Section
  • Sewing Machine — Parts & Functions
  • Industrial vs Domestic Sewing Machines
  • Threading & Machine Maintenance
  • Basic Hand Stitches
  • Fabric Identification & Handling
2. Basic Machine Operations (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Straight Stitching & Seams
  • Overlock & Flatlock Machine Operations
  • Buttonhole & Button Stitching Machines
  • Hemming & Edge Finishing
  • Operation of Special Sewing Attachments
3. Garment Stitching (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Stitching of Shirts & Trousers
  • Stitching of Salwar Kameez & Kurti
  • Stitching of Children's Wear
  • Pocket, Collar & Cuff Attachment
  • Zipper & Fastener Application
4. Apparel Production & Quality (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Cutting Room Practices
  • Assembly Line Production Process
  • Quality Checking & Defect Identification
  • Ironing, Pressing & Packing
  • Garment Costing & Production Planning
5. Entrepreneurship & Maintenance (All Semesters)
  • Sewing Unit Setup & Management
  • Maintenance of Sewing Machines
  • Order Taking & Customer Service
  • Marketing of Stitched Products
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
