Admission Process

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in COPA.

Apply Online at scvtup.in →
1
Visit scvtup.in — official UP ITI admission portal
2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose COPA as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
