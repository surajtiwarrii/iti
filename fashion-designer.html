NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Fashion Designing & Garment Making

Fashion Designer trade trains students in garment design, pattern making, sewing, embroidery and fashion illustration — preparing them for jobs in the textile and garment industry, boutiques and self-employment.

Eligibility
8th Pass
Duration
1 Year
Fees
₹780 / Year
Certification
NCVT / SCVT
Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basics of Fashion & Textiles (Semester 1)
  • Introduction to Fashion Designing Trade
  • Tools & Equipment Used in Garment Making
  • Fabric Types & Properties
  • Measurement Taking Techniques
  • Basic Hand Stitches & Sewing Machine Operation
  • Workplace Safety & Maintenance
2. Pattern Making & Drafting (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Drafting of Basic Blocks (Bodice, Sleeve, Skirt)
  • Pattern Making for Salwar, Kurti & Blouse
  • Pattern Grading Basics
  • Layout & Fabric Cutting Techniques
  • Marker Making for Cost Saving
3. Garment Construction (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Construction of Salwar Kameez
  • Construction of Kurti & Blouse
  • Construction of Shirts & Trousers
  • Finishing Techniques — Buttons, Hooks, Zippers
  • Quality Checking of Garments
4. Embroidery & Fashion Illustration (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Hand Embroidery Techniques
  • Machine Embroidery Basics
  • Fashion Illustration & Sketching
  • Surface Ornamentation (Sequins, Beads, Applique)
  • Trend Analysis & Design Development
  • Computer-Aided Design (CAD) for Fashion — Basics
5. Boutique & Entrepreneurship (All Semesters)
  • Boutique Setup & Management
  • Costing & Pricing of Garments
  • Customer Measurement & Order Handling
  • Marketing of Fashion Products
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy

  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics

  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
