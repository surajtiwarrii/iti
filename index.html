About Us

About the Institute

Government Industrial Training Institute, Karnailganj, Gonda is a premier government vocational training institution under the Uttar Pradesh Skill Development Mission. Located in Karnailganj, Gonda district, the institute is committed to empowering youth with industry-relevant technical skills.

Under the National Council for Vocational Training (NCVT) framework, the institute offers structured training in 11 trades, preparing students for employment and entrepreneurship across diverse sectors.

✓
NCVT & SCVT certified courses with nationally recognised certification
✓
Experienced and qualified instructors in each trade
✓
Modern workshops and practical training infrastructure
✓
Placement assistance and apprenticeship support
