NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Plumbing & Sanitation

Plumber trade trains students in water supply systems, drainage, pipe fitting and sanitation installations — preparing them for jobs in construction, maintenance and self-employment as plumbing contractors.

Duration

Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basic Plumbing Practice (Semester 1)
  • Safety Precautions & PPE
  • Plumber's Hand Tools & Equipment
  • Pipe Materials — GI, PVC, CPVC, PPR
  • Pipe Cutting, Threading & Joining
  • Pipe Fittings & Their Uses
  • Measurement & Marking Techniques
2. Water Supply Systems (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Cold Water Supply Systems
  • Hot Water Supply Systems & Geysers
  • Overhead Tanks & Underground Sumps
  • Pumps — Types, Installation & Maintenance
  • Water Meters & Valves
  • Pipe Insulation Techniques
3. Sanitation & Drainage (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Sanitary Fittings — WC, Wash Basin, Sink, Urinals
  • Drainage System Layout
  • Soil & Waste Pipe Installation
  • Traps, Manholes & Inspection Chambers
  • Septic Tank & Soak Pit Construction
  • Ventilation of Drainage Systems
4. Advanced Plumbing & Maintenance (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Solar Water Heating Systems
  • Fire Fighting Pipe Systems Basics
  • Rainwater Harvesting Systems
  • Leak Detection & Pipe Repair
  • Reading Plumbing Drawings & Layouts
  • Estimation & Costing of Plumbing Work
5. Tools, Safety & Practice (All Semesters)
  • Use of Power Tools in Plumbing
  • Welding/Brazing for Pipe Joints
  • Quality Standards (BIS) for Plumbing Materials
  • Maintenance Schedules & Record Keeping
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy
  • Basics of Computer
  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety

Entrepreneurship

Quality & Productivity

  • ISO Basics
