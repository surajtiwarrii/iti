NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Precision Machining & Turning

Turner trade trains students in lathe machine operations, metal turning, shaping and precision machining of components — preparing them for jobs in manufacturing, tool rooms and CNC machine industries.

Eligibility
10th Pass
Duration
2 Years
Fees
₹780 / Year
Certification

Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basic Workshop Practice (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Safety Practices & PPE
  • Hand Tools & Their Uses
  • Marking & Measuring Instruments
  • Filing, Chipping & Fitting Practice
  • Material Identification & Properties
  • Basic Bench Work
2. Lathe Machine Operations (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Lathe Machine — Parts & Functions
  • Cutting Tools & Tool Geometry
  • Plain Turning & Facing
  • Step Turning & Taper Turning
  • Thread Cutting on Lathe
  • Knurling, Grooving & Forming
  • Drilling & Boring on Lathe
3. Precision Machining (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Center Drilling & Centering Work
  • Eccentric Turning
  • Internal & External Thread Cutting
  • Use of Chucks, Faceplates & Mandrels
  • Tolerances, Fits & Limits
  • Surface Finish & Quality Checking
4. Other Machine Tools & CNC (Semester 4)
  • Milling Machine — Basic Operations
  • Shaper & Planer Machine Basics
  • Grinding Machine Operations
  • CNC Lathe — Introduction & Programming Basics
  • CAD/CAM Awareness
  • Maintenance of Machine Tools
5. Quality & Production (All Semesters)
  • Reading Engineering Drawings
  • Use of Precision Measuring Instruments (Vernier, Micrometer)
  • Quality Control in Machining
  • Production Planning Basics
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy

  • MS Office Basics

  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation

Admission Process

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in Turner.

Apply Online at scvtup.in →
1
Visit scvtup.in — official UP ITI admission portal
2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose Turner as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
