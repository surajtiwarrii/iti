NCVT Certified Course

Build Your Career in Welding & Metal Fabrication

Welder trade trains students in gas welding, arc welding, TIG/MIG welding and metal fabrication techniques — preparing them for jobs in fabrication units, construction, shipbuilding, and pipeline industries.

Eligibility
8th Pass
Duration
1 Year
Fees

Certification

Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basic Workshop Practice (Semester 1)
  • Introduction to Welding Trade & Safety
  • Hand Tools & Their Uses
  • Marking, Measuring & Cutting Tools
  • Sheet Metal Operations
  • Basic Fitting Practice
  • Personal Protective Equipment (PPE)
  • Fire Safety in Welding Shop
2. Gas Welding & Cutting (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Oxy-Acetylene Gas Welding
  • Gas Cutting Process & Equipment
  • Brazing & Soldering
  • Flame Setting & Adjustment
  • Welding of Mild Steel Plates
  • Edge Preparation Techniques
3. Arc Welding (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Shielded Metal Arc Welding (SMAW)
  • Electrode Selection & Classification
  • Welding Positions (1G, 2G, 3G, 4G)
  • Butt & Fillet Joint Welding
  • Welding Defects, Causes & Remedies
  • Welding of Pipes & Plates
  • Arc Welding Machine Maintenance
4. TIG & MIG/MAG Welding (Semester 3 & 4)
  • TIG (GTAW) Welding Process
  • MIG/MAG (GMAW) Welding Process
  • Welding of Stainless Steel & Aluminium
  • Welding of Non-Ferrous Metals
  • Welding Symbols & Drawing Interpretation
  • Destructive & Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) Basics
  • Submerged Arc Welding (SAW) Overview
  • CNC / Robotic Welding Awareness
5. Metal Fabrication & Quality (All Semesters)
  • Sheet Metal Fabrication Jobs
  • Pipe Fabrication & Joining
  • Structural Fabrication Basics
  • Welding Inspection & Quality Standards
  • Pre-heating & Post-weld Heat Treatment
  • Costing & Estimation of Welding Jobs
5. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy
  • Basics of Computer
  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety
  • Workshop Safety Rules
  • Fire Safety & First Aid
  • PPE Usage
  • Housekeeping (5S)
Entrepreneurship
  • Business Setup Basics
  • Customer Service
  • Self-Employment Planning
Quality & Productivity
  • Quality Control Concepts
  • 5S Housekeeping
  • ISO Basics

Admission Process

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in Welder.

1
Visit scvtup.in — official UP ITI admission portal
2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose Welder as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
