Build Your Career in Electronics & Communication

Electronics trade trains students in electronic circuits, PCB design, consumer electronics repair, embedded systems and industrial control — preparing them for jobs in electronics manufacturing, servicing and self-employment.

Course Curriculum

Syllabus

1. Basic Electronics & Components (Semester 1)
  • Safety Practices in Electronics Lab
  • Soldering & De-soldering Techniques
  • Resistors, Capacitors & Inductors
  • Diodes, Transistors & Rectifiers
  • Power Supplies (SMPS, Regulated PSU)
  • Use of Multimeter, CRO & Function Generator
2. Consumer Electronics (Semester 1 & 2)
  • Audio & Video Systems
  • Television (LED/LCD) Repair
  • Mobile Phone Servicing Basics
  • Home Appliances Electronics (Washing Machine, Microwave)
  • Amplifiers & Sound Systems
  • Fault Finding in Consumer Devices
3. Digital Electronics & PCB (Semester 2 & 3)
  • Number Systems & Logic Gates
  • Combinational & Sequential Circuits
  • PCB Design & Fabrication
  • Microcontroller Basics (8051 / Arduino)
  • Sensors & Transducers
  • Embedded System Programming Basics
4. Industrial Electronics & Communication (Semester 3 & 4)
  • Industrial Control Circuits
  • PLC & Automation Basics
  • CCTV Installation & Networking
  • Communication Systems — Wired & Wireless
  • Satellite & DTH Systems
  • Solar Power Electronics
  • Computer Hardware & Peripherals
5. Testing & Maintenance (All Semesters)
  • Use of Testing & Measuring Instruments
  • Troubleshooting Electronic Circuits
  • Maintenance of Electronic Equipment
  • Repair Documentation & Service Records
6. Employability Skills (All Semesters)
IT Literacy
  • Basics of Computer
  • Internet Usage
  • MS Office Basics
Communication Skills
  • Spoken English
  • Interview Preparation
  • Behavioral Skills
Occupational Safety
  • Workshop Safety Rules
  • Fire Safety & First Aid
  • PPE Usage
  • Housekeeping (5S)
Entrepreneurship
  • Business Setup Basics
  • Customer Service
  • Self-Employment Planning
Quality & Productivity
  • Quality Control Concepts
  • 5S Housekeeping
  • ISO Basics

Admission Process

Admission 2026-27

Admissions are conducted through the UP ITI Online Admission Portal (SCVT UP). Seats are limited and filled on merit basis. Apply before the last date to secure your seat in Electronics.

Apply Online at scvtup.in →
1
Visit scvtup.in — official UP ITI admission portal
2
Register with Aadhaar, fill personal & academic details
3
Select ITI Karnailganj, Gonda and choose Electronics as preferred trade
4
Pay application fee and submit the form online
5
Check merit list and report for document verification
