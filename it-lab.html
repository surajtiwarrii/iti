IT Lab — Digital Skills for Every Trade

The IT Lab at Government ITI Karnailganj, Gonda is dedicated to building computer literacy and digital skills among all trainees. It is used for IT Literacy & Information Technology System (ITS-IT) classes that form part of the Employability Skills curriculum for every trade.
